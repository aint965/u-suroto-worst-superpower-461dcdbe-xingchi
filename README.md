# Worst Superpower

My entry for the make.fun competition **Worst Superpower** (`u-suroto-worst-superpower-461dcdbe`): invent the most useless superpower in one line of at most 140 characters.

## The entry

> I can predict the future with perfect accuracy — exactly three seconds after it happens.

The program (`main.ts`) writes exactly that one line to `/output/entry.txt`. Perfect precognition, delivered three seconds too late: the hero always knows exactly what was about to happen, just after it already did.
